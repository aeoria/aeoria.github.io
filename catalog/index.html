Field tools for trades

Offline iPhone calculators for electricians, plumbers and heating installers, drywall and tile crews, deck and stair builders, contractors and real estate photographers. Each one does its core job for free and sells a one-time Pro unlock for job lists, PDFs and multi-room work. Each site also carries how-to guides and printable field checklists that are useful on their own, with or without the app.

Box Fill Check

boxfillcheck.aeoriaapps.com
ElectriciansNEC 314.16iPhoneOffline

An NEC electrical box fill calculator for electricians and inspectors. You pick the box, add conductors, devices, clamps and grounds, and the app tells you right away whether the box passes, how much spare volume is left and how many more wires would fit. It answers the question every rough-in raises: will the wires fit this box?

Free

  • NEC 2017, 2020 and 2023 edition picker.
  • Standard metal boxes from Table 314.16(A), or the volume stamped on the box.
  • Smallest passing box picker and history of the last 50 boxes.

Pro, one-time unlock

  • Whole job by rooms with a box list and pass count.
  • Plaster and extension rings using stamped volume.
  • Inspector PDF box schedule with code edition and date; templates such as kitchen GFCI, double switch and 4-11/16 splice.
  • Mexico (NOM-001-SEDE) mode using NEC 2017 box fill rules.

Conduit percent fill and Canada CEC mode are deliberately not in this version.

Fault Current & SCCR Label

sccr.aeoriaapps.com
ElectriciansNEC 110.24iPhoneOffline

An available fault current calculator for commercial electrical work. Start from a utility letter or an infinite bus, enter the transformer kVA, secondary voltage and %Z, add the feeder run, and the app gives you available fault current at the transformer terminals and at the equipment, in amps and kA. It then compares that number with the equipment's AIC or SCCR and shows pass or fail, so you can prepare the NEC 110.24 field marking.

Free

  • One calculation point with one feeder (material, raceway, size, length, parallel sets).
  • Optional impedance tolerance for a worst-case result.
  • One AIC or SCCR comparison with pass or fail.

Pro, one-time unlock

  • Unlimited point tree from the main panel to subpanels to equipment, with motor contribution.
  • Continue downstream from a known current in a utility letter or a measured value.
  • Printable NEC 110.24 labels (4x2 in, 4x3 in, A6), an equipment list with SCCR status, and a method PDF for the inspector.

A field aid, not an engineering stamp.

CarpentryDecks & basementsiPhoneOffline

Starlo: mock interview practice

mockinterview.aeoriaapps.com
Job seekersSTAR methodVoiceiPhone

A voice mock interview app. You answer interview questions out loud, and Starlo breaks your answer into the four STAR parts (Situation, Task, Action, Result) so you can see which one is missing or weak. Retry just that part and compare attempts side by side to hear the second take improve.

What it does

  • Questions for your role, or generated from a pasted job post.
  • Full mock interviews with follow-up questions based on what you said.
  • One-way interview practice with think time and a countdown; MMI-style stations.
  • Delivery check for pace, filler words and answer length, plus a plan up to interview day.

A practice tool only; not a job offer or admissions advice.
